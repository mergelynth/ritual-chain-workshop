# Reflection

A fair bounty system should expose only the information that is necessary at each stage of the process. During the submission phase, answers should remain hidden so participants cannot copy or improve upon someone else's work. After the submission period ends, answers can be revealed and evaluated transparently. AI is well suited for ranking submissions consistently against a predefined rubric, but it should act as an advisor rather than the final authority. The final payout decision should remain under human control, allowing the bounty owner to review the AI's recommendation before distributing funds. This combination of private submissions, transparent evaluation, and human oversight provides a balance between fairness, transparency, and accountability. For even stronger privacy guarantees, a future version could leverage Ritual's Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) so that submissions remain encrypted until the AI evaluation is complete.
